## Frozen attributes and codebooks

Per municipality take median of 12 monthly logTOTAL values and the five separate monthly percentages in2023. On each train fold fit five ridge regressions with penalty10, unpenalized intercept, standardized logTOTAL and its square plus observed administrative-type indicators. Do not include region indicators: the primary split holds whole regions out. Unknown statuses have zero effect plus an explicit flag. Conditional coordinates are percentage residuals, centered by training median and divided by training IQR. A zero-IQR coordinate is omitted and recorded. Labor coordinates are log(jobs/residents) and the observed O/P/Q share, likewise training median/IQR. Spending and labor blocks receive squared Euclidean weights0.5 each: divide each by sqrt(active dimension count), multiply by sqrt(0.5). No selected weights.

Store original fitted labels and nearest-center projections separately. Quarterly sensitivity keeps the observed annual2023 labor coordinates fixed and changes only the spending annual aggregation. Report exact zero-change constant fixture.

An admissible descriptive four-type model has every group >=5% of train cohort and represented in>=5 regions in full fit and each outer train fit. Assess seed sensitivity with seeds20261009..20261018; no best-seed selection. Report ARI distribution against the prespecified first seed and all group sizes. Predictors are ridge penalty10 with unpenalized intercept; train-standardize continuous design columns, missing covariates train-median+indicator where allowed. Controls: logTOTAL2023, logpopulation2023, administrative type. Define continuous7 as the five conditional coordinates plus both labor coordinates. Models: PERSIST=wage23 unchanged in log space; GROWTH=wage23+mean(train logwage24−logwage23); C=controls; C4=C+four attribute-codebook indicators; CG=C+four graph-prototype projection indicators; FULL=C+continuous7; QUAD=FULL with all continuous squares/pairwise interactions; PFULL=FULL+logwage23; PQUAD=QUAD+logwage23; PFULL4=PFULL+attribute-type indicators; PQUAD4=PQUAD+attribute-type indicators; PQUADG=PQUAD+graph-projection indicators.
